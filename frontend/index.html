> 01_ARRIVAL

Eighty-six billion neurons, and one quiet keyboard.

Everything you think passes through a neuromuscular system that translates thought into typing. CogniDiff continuously monitors the rhythm of that translation — never the words — learning what normal looks like for you.

> 02_THE_BASELINE

Your baseline is you, not an average.

A fast typist and a deliberate typist are both normal. CogniDiff rejects rigid demographic averages, building an individualized statistical baseline from 14 days of everyday typing, hour by hour, to detect meaningful deviations from your own normal.

  • CALIBRATION 14-DAY ROLLING
  • QUALITY >60 FILTERED
  • WINDOW 14 DAYS

> 04_PRIVACY_ARCHITECTURE

Rhythm and timing. Never the words.

CogniDiff extracts an 8-feature behavioral timing vector locally inside the browser extension. How long keys are held, gaps before the next key, and correction episodes. Your typed text never leaves your browser, because it is never recorded in the first place.

  • RAW TEXT NEVER STORED
  • KEY VALUES NEVER STORED
  • VECTORS 8 FEATURES

> 05_EXPLAINABLE_AI

A score is useless until it tells you why.

A CogniScore on its own invites confusion or panic. TreeExplainer SHAP values break down every deviation into transparent, plain-English factors: your pauses are 21% longer than usual, corrections are up 14%, and typing rhythm shifted — in words you and your clinician can verify.

  • METRIC COGNISCORE 0-100
  • EXPLAINER SHAP TREE ATTR.
  • OUTPUT TOP 3 CHANGES

> 06_DUAL_CONFIRMATION

One bad night is not a signal.

CogniDiff refuses to alarm on a single fatigued or stressful session. It flags concern only when statistical Z-score deviation and unsupervised Isolation Forest agree across multi-day sessions, holding false-positive rates below 15% through context-aware filtering.

  • ANOMALY ISOLATION FOREST
  • VALIDATION DUAL PIPELINE
  • FALSE POSITIVES <15%

> 07_CLINICAL_INTEGRITY

Detection of change. Never a diagnosis.

CogniDiff detects subtle deviations from an individual's own typing baseline to support longitudinal health awareness. It is a screening and monitoring tool, not a diagnostic device — empowering early, objective conversations with qualified healthcare professionals.
