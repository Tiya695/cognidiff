CogniDiff

> NEURAL RHYTHM MONITOR

Before anything is captured, read this.

CogniDiff measures the rhythm of your typing: how long you hold each key, the gaps between keystrokes, how often you delete and retype. From that it learns what normal looks like for you, and tells you when it changes.

It never records what you type. Not the words, not the characters, not a single letter.

What gets stored

Captured

  • Time between keystrokes, in milliseconds
  • How long each key was held down
  • Counts: total keys, backspaces, pauses
  • A key category only: letter, digit, space, backspace, punctuation

Never captured

  • The characters you type
  • Words, sentences, messages, searches
  • Passwords, card numbers, one-time codes
  • Page contents, URLs you visit, screenshots

"hello world" and "abcde fghij" produce an identical record. The content is not stored in a weaker form, it is not stored at all.

Where it runs is your choice

CogniDiff ships with access to no websites whatsoever. It cannot see any page until you approve that specific site, and Chrome itself will ask you before each one.

Approve the places you do ordinary writing, a documents app or a notes app. Everywhere else stays invisible to it.

You can check and revoke every approval at any time, from the extension popup or from chrome://extensions.

Capture stops on its own

Even on a site you approved, nothing is recorded when:

  • you are typing in a password field
  • the field is a card number, CVV or one-time code
  • the field sits in a form that contains a password
  • the page looks like banking, payment, health or sign-in
  • you are in an incognito window

If one of these appears midway through a minute, the partial recording is deleted rather than sent.

How the score works

Your browser groups typing into one-minute batches. Those are held on your own machine and, once you sign in, synced to your CogniDiff account. Once a day the account works out a CogniScore: how far that day sat from your own established baseline.

It needs roughly two weeks of ordinary typing before the baseline means anything. Until then you will see it collecting, not scoring.

CogniDiff detects deviation from your own typing baseline. It does not detect, diagnose or confirm cognitive decline, and no clinical accuracy is claimed. Discuss any concern with a qualified healthcare professional.

Monitoring stays off until you switch it on. Nothing has been captured yet.
